Privacy

Last updated: 2026-05-16

What we collect

When you submit a supply list through the Bulk Supply Counter form on the homepage, we collect the information you provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Optional: phone number and business name
  • Buyer type (personal / mobile detailing / shop / resale / etc.)
  • Product categories you're interested in
  • Quantity or volume notes
  • Preferred contact method
  • Any notes or supply list you paste in

When you email hello@smartautocareproducts.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

How we use it

We use your information for one purpose: to reply to your supply list inquiry with pricing, availability, lead times, and bundle options. That's the only thing we do with it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties.
  • We don't add you to marketing lists you didn't opt into.
  • We don't use third-party advertising trackers on this site.

Storage

Inquiries are stored in our email system for as long as needed to fulfill or follow up on the inquiry. You can request deletion of your information at any time by emailing hello@smartautocareproducts.com.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses only essential cookies needed for the page to function. We don't currently use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we'll update this page first.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from people under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your information: hello@smartautocareproducts.com.
