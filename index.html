FAQ

Common questions from buyers.

Don't see yours? Send it through the supply form — we read every one.

Q.01 Do you sell to regular car owners?

Yes. Anyone serious about taking care of their vehicle can buy from us. Start with the Weekend Wash Kit or browse the Loadout Wall.

Q.02 Do you offer bulk pricing?

Yes. Bulk pricing is available for shops, mobile detailers, swap-meet sellers, and resellers. Send a supply list with quantities and we'll quote it.

Q.03 Can mobile detailers order kits?

Yes. The Mobile Detailer Kit is built around what fits in a van or hatch and covers a full job: wash, decontaminate, correct, protect, interior, finish.

Q.04 Can shops request recurring supply orders?

Yes. Body shops and detail shops can set up recurring restock through the Bulk Supply Counter — choose a cadence and a category mix and we'll quote ongoing pricing.

Q.05 Do you offer reseller bundles?

Yes. The Reseller / Swap Meet Starter Kit is a bundle priced for resale margin. Tell us your booth size and target price points and we'll match a bundle.

Q.06 Is this a full ecommerce store yet?

Not yet. The catalog is opening in waves — some products are already shippable, others are quote-based. Use the Bulk Supply Counter to get pricing for what you need today.

Q.07 Can I send a custom supply list?

Yes — that's the fastest path. Paste your list into the Bulk Supply Counter form with quantities, and we'll come back with pricing and lead times.
