For · Car Owners

The wash, done right at home.

You don't need ten bottles of the same thing. You need a tight, honest set of products that work on your driveway every weekend — and a clear path to step up when you're ready.

What you typically need

Owners landing here usually fall into one of three buckets: weekend washers who want a safe, swirl-free routine; enthusiasts ready to add decontamination and protection; or new-car owners who want a ceramic-grade finish maintained properly.

Whichever bucket you're in, the smart starting point isn't the most expensive product — it's the right wash mitt, a pH-neutral soap, and a drying towel that doesn't induce marring. Build from there.

If you've ever bought a "kit" from a big-box store and ended up with three products you don't use, you'll recognize what we're avoiding here.

Best starting kit

The Weekend Wash Kit covers everything for a complete safe wash, with a clear upgrade path when you want more.

  • Wash mitt & grit guard
  • Drying towel set
  • Spray sealant or wax
