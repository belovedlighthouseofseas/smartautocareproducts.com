For · Mobile Detailers

Built for the truck, the van, the hatch.

You're not stocking a shop — you're stocking a vehicle. Every product has to earn its space, work in different conditions, and let you finish a full appointment without a second trip.

What a mobile loadout actually needs

The right loadout covers a full appointment from wash → decon → correct → protect → interior without overloading the cargo area. That usually means a foam cannon, a single all-arounder polish, a ceramic spray sealant, and a graded microfiber pack — not four polishes you'll never use on a driveway job.

Bottle sizes matter too. 16-oz refill bottles travel and pour better than gallons in the back of a van; gallons go in the shop refill station you keep at home base.

Send us your typical job mix (number of cars per week, types of vehicles, how often you do paint correction vs. maintenance washes) and we'll match a kit and a refill cadence to it.
