For · Resellers & Swap Meet Sellers

Bundles built to move.

If you're working a swap meet booth or reselling out of a van, you don't need our whole catalog — you need the four or five SKUs people actually pick up off the table. Priced for margin, packed for booth.

What actually moves at booths

Booth and swap-meet sales lean toward visible-value SKUs: microfiber multi-packs (everyone needs them, easy to demo), spray bottle assortments, interior detailer that smells right, and a single entry-level ceramic or sealant kit per booth.

Boring categories (compound, prep wipes, shop towels) don't move at booths — they belong in shop restock orders. We'll steer you away from stocking those for resale.

Tell us your booth size, target retail price points, and the demographics of your foot traffic. We'll match a starter bundle that fits both the table and the customer.
