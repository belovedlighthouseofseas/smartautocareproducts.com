For · Body & Detail Shops

Predictable restock. One supplier.

If you're running a body shop, a detail shop, or a repair business, the supply problem isn't variety — it's consistency. Same SKUs, same cadence, predictable cost. That's what we're here to lock in.

The restock relationship

The Shop Restock Kit isn't a one-shot order — it's a cadence. We agree on the SKUs and quantities you go through in a typical month or quarter, and we ship them on a schedule. Pricing stays consistent. You stop ordering line-by-line.

Most shops we work with have a mix that looks roughly like: graded microfiber in volume, compound and polish refilled monthly, refill spray bottles and triggers, masking tape and prep supplies, shop towels and nitrile gloves. We can adjust to your mix.

If you want to add or swap categories at any time — say you start offering ceramic packages and need to add coating kits — just tell us. We'll fold it into the next shipment.
