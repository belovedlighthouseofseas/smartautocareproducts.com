What this lane covers

Protection is the layer between the finish and everything trying to wear it down: water spots, road salt, UV, bird droppings, brake dust, bug residue, sap. The products in this lane create a sacrificial barrier so the paint, glass, wheel coatings, and trim wear down slower than they otherwise would.

The three formats you'll see most often:

  • 9H ceramic coatings — long-life (one to several years), requires panel prep, semi-permanent until polished off. Best for cars you intend to keep.
  • Sealants — synthetic, durable, easy-on. Sit in the middle: more durable than wax, less commitment than ceramic.
  • Spray protectants — quick-apply, short duration. Best as a top-up between deeper protection refreshes, or for cars washed weekly.

You don't need all three. For most owners, picking one protection format and maintaining it well beats stacking three products and getting confused about reapplication schedules.

How to pick

If you keep the car: go ceramic. Higher prep effort, lowest long-term hassle.

If you wash weekly: spray sealant works fine — top up every few washes.

If you sell cars often: sealant. Quick to apply, looks great immediately, doesn't lock in for years.

If you're a mobile detailer: stock all three at different price points. Customer pays for the level they want.

Shop note

Protection only sticks to a properly prepped surface. Skip the prep and even the best ceramic coating will streak, fail, or pull off in months. The Correction Lane covers what to use for prep before applying anything in this lane.

Common items quoted in this lane

  • 9H ceramic coating kits (1-year, 2-year)
  • Spray sealants and topper kits
  • Glass-specific hydrophobic coatings
  • Wheel and brake-dust-repellent coatings
  • Trim restorer / UV block
  • Application applicators, suede squares, prep panel wipes
