What this lane covers

The Bulk Restock lane is the working backbone of any shop or mobile setup. These aren't the SKUs that win customers — they're the SKUs that make sure you don't run out mid-job. Microfiber by the case, refill spray bottles in the right sizes, masking tape that doesn't lift paint, shop towels that don't shed, nitrile gloves that fit.

The categories:

  • Microfiber packs — graded by GSM and blend. Bulk pricing matters; everyone goes through these.
  • Spray bottles & refill containers — chemical-resistant, calibrated. Stocked in 8-oz, 16-oz, and gallon sizes.
  • Masking tape — automotive-grade; not the hardware-store roll.
  • Shop towels — disposable or washable, depending on shop preference.
  • Nitrile gloves — chemical resistance, properly sized.
  • Polishing & correction consumables — pads, applicator blocks, suedes by the case.

How to pick

Mobile detailer: microfiber multi-pack (graded by use), 16-oz refill bottles, basic gloves. Buy seasonally based on your busy months.

Body shop / detail shop: bulk microfiber by the case, gallon refill containers, masking tape in standard widths, gloves by the box. Set a recurring cadence.

Reseller: microfiber multi-packs are some of the easiest-moving booth SKUs. Stock the visible-value pack sizes.

Shop note

Microfiber goes through three lifecycle stages: new (use for buffing, drying, ceramic), broken-in (general detailing), damaged (wheels and engine bay only — never back to paint). Label your bins. Bringing a damaged towel back to paint causes more marring than washing without one.

Common items quoted in this lane

  • Microfiber towel packs (12-pack, 24-pack, by case)
  • Drying towels (waffle / twist-loop)
  • Refill spray bottles (8-oz, 16-oz, 32-oz)
  • Chemical-resistant triggers
  • Gallon refill containers
  • Automotive masking tape
  • Nitrile gloves (sizing)
  • Foam applicator blocks (case)
