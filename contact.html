When to email vs. when to use the supply form

Use the supply form when: you have a list of products and quantities, you want pricing, you want a bundle quote, you want to set up recurring restock.

Email us directly when: you have a partnership idea, you want to ask about a specific product before buying, you're a media/press inquiry, you have a question about a previous order, or you're not sure what you need yet and want a recommendation.
